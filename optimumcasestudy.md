The Need: Building a Seamless SMB Wi-Fi Experience
Optimum, a part of Altice USA, needed to deliver a high-value, plug-and-play Wi-Fi solution to its large base of small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs). Each location required secure private Wi-Fi for employees, from coffee shops and medical offices to car dealerships, plus a branded guest portal. On top of that, Optimum wanted to introduce a marketing component so SMB owners could capture customer analytics and run promotions—without burdening local staff with complex technical tasks.

To achieve this, Optimum teamed up with reseller’s hosting and managed services—and, behind the scenes, leveraged WiBUZ’s API-first wibipOS platform as the digital “engine.” This solution is integrated directly with Optimum’s internal provisioning system (AMDOCS), ensuring each new customer site is automatically configured, fully branded, and ready to go when an Optimum technician leaves the premises.

The Challenges
Minimal On-Site Setup: SMB owners do not have IT staff to handle Wi-Fi complexities. Optimum needed a hands-off approach for local managers, requiring only straightforward tasks for technicians.
Brand & Marketing Portal: Each business wanted a unique splash page and data collection features—especially for guest Wi-Fi sessions.
Automated Provisioning: With over 350,000 small businesses in Altice’s coverage, manually configuring SSIDs and captive portals for each client was impossible to scale. A fully automated workflow was mandatory.
Actionable Insights: SMBs often lack advanced marketing tools. Optimum saw an opportunity to offer targeted campaigns and demographic analytics through the Wi-Fi portal.
The WiBUZ-Enabled Reseller/ Optimum Solution
Single Pane of Glass & API-First Core
The wibipOS platform integrates with AMDOCS provisioning so that each new site triggers an automated config push: SSIDs, captive portal templates, subscriber-level VLANs, bandwidth policies, and more.
The reseller hosts the physical NOC and infrastructure, providing Tier 2 support. Optimum’s Tier 1 staff use a single portal to see all SMB networks, usage analytics, and device health.
Live SDK Team & Dynamic Roadmap
WiBUZ’s embedded engineers (the “Live SDK” team) collaborate with the reseller to quickly add new features or fix issues that pop up in the field.
Because wibipOS is modular, Optimum can request new marketing tools, additional SSIDs, or advanced security filters and deploy them to thousands of locations without rewriting everything from scratch.
Automated On-Site Turn-up
An Optimum technician arrives with up to three access points, a switch, and the “virtualized managed edge” device.
As soon as they power it on, wibipOS automatically downloads and applies the correct configuration. Within minutes, the location broadcasts the correct SSIDs, and a welcome email and a marketing-ready portal are sent to the SMB admin.
Built-In Marketing Engine
Pro Wi-Fi (Optimum’s brand name) allows SMBs to:
Create custom login screens, upload logos, and choose color schemes.
Capture visitors’ demographics, contact details, and session data for future campaigns.
Manage private and guest networks with separate usage and security policies.
This robust marketing feature set is powered by wibipOS in the background, which provides easily accessible dashboards, analytics, and surveys.
Pro Wi-Fi: The Marketing Engine in Action
Intuitive Guest Portal: SMB owners select templates, upload logos, and tweak the color theme—no coding required.
Smart Marketing Dashboard: A clean UI displays real-time session counts, visitor demographics, and usage trends.
Guest Capture & Surveys: Visitors log in with a personalized splash page and may answer short surveys, which feed contact information and analytics into an SMB’s marketing database.
Secure Private Wi-Fi: Employees connect to a separate, password-protected SSID with advanced isolation and optional URL filtering for compliance and peace of mind.
24/7/365 Remote Management: Reseller’s NOC monitors networks, while any deeper or new feature requests are funneled back to WiBUZ via the Live SDK for quick resolution.
