## 2. How wibipOS Works: The API-First Architecture

wibipOS is **API-first**. If your solution has APIs, we ingest and mirror those capabilities into a single pane of glass—one login, one interface, one workflow. The controller becomes an engine that powers wibipOS, invisible to the operator.

### The Three-Layer Stack

| Layer | Component | Role |
|-------|-----------|------|
| **Business Layer** | wibipOS | Multi-tenant MSP workflows, billing & CRM integration, branded portals, business analytics, campaign management. The interface MSPs actually use. |
| **Controller Layer** | NetExperience / ecOpen | Cloud-native OpenWiFi control, AP provisioning, RRM, firmware management. The engine that powers the network. Hidden from the operator. |
| **Hardware Layer** | Edgecore | OpenWiFi APs (Wi-Fi 6/6E/7), OpenLAN switches. Pre-configured, ready to rack. The foundation. |

### Key Insight: Controller Agnosticism

wibipOS doesn't care which controller sits beneath it. The MSP's operational experience is identical whether the backend is NetExperience, ecOpen, or legacy Ruckus. This solves the channel conflict:

- Edgecore sells hardware regardless of controller choice
- NetExperience keeps their controller position without competing with ecOpen at the operator layer
- MSPs get a unified interface and stop asking "what am I supposed to use?"

## 8. Partner FAQ

### Q: Does wibipOS replace NetExperience?

**A:** No. NetExperience is the OpenWiFi controller (the "engine"). wibipOS is the business operations layer (the "dashboard and tooling"). They stack. The controller powers the network; wibipOS powers the MSP's business workflows.

### Q: Does wibipOS compete with Edgecore's ecCLOUD?

**A:** No. ecCLOUD is a network management controller. wibipOS is a business operations platform. ecCLOUD (or NetExperience, or ecOpen) runs beneath wibipOS as the network control layer. wibipOS is controller-agnostic—it works with any of them.

### Q: What if the customer only has Edgecore hardware?

**A:** wibipOS still adds value through tenant isolation, captive portals, campaigns, and role-based access. The multi-vendor capability becomes valuable when they expand or acquire sites with legacy gear.

### Q: What if a customer needs a feature wibipOS doesn't have?

**A:** That's what the Live SDK Team is for. 25 R&D engineers build custom integrations on demand. The customer gets their feature on their timeline—not waiting for a vendor release cycle.

### Q: What about customers with legacy Ruckus/Cisco gear?

**A:** BYOTS—**Bring Your Own Tech Stack.** wibipOS integrates legacy gear via API adapters. Their existing hardware isn't debt—it becomes a managed asset under the same dashboard as new Edgecore equipment. This eliminates the "rip and replace" objection that kills OpenWiFi deals.

### Q: What's the deployment model?

**A:** wibipOS is cloud-hosted. MSPs get a single instance with multi-tenant capabilities. No on-prem installation required. New partner: 2 weeks to first customer deployment.
