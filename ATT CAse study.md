The Need: Building a TurnKey Wi-Fi Experience for MDUs
AT&T, a major telco, needed a comprehensive, turnkey solution to deliver robust wired and wireless broadband services in multi-dwelling units (MDUs), such as apartments, mixed-use complexes, and student housing. These properties often require high-performance connectivity for both private residences and shared amenities. Residents expect 10–50 Mbps per device as a baseline, making reliable, fast Wi-Fi as crucial as any other utility.

The Challenges
Shared Infrastructure, High Expectations
MDU tenants demand stable, high-speed connectivity. However, each property has a unique layout, capacity needs, and density challenges that complicate traditional network deployments.
Bulk Connectivity at Scale
AT&T sought a partner capable of delivering a complete solution—covering everything from in-unit coverage to public areas—while maintaining a consistent user experience.
Brand & Integration Requirements
The solution had to be white-labeled under AT&T’s brand, but it still incorporated advanced features and remained ready for future expansions, such as additional IoT or property management integrations.
The reseller for WiBUZ, leveraged WiBUZ’s core platform and engineering expertise to fulfill AT&T’s stringent requirements. From behind the scenes, WiBUZ provided the single-pane-of-glass, multi-tenant management system, plus an API-first architecture to support new hardware and software integrations with minimal disruption.

WiBUZ’s Live SDK team was key to the collaboration, embedded with reseller’s service delivery. This allowed rapid feature development, continuous customizations, and a Dynamic Roadmap approach. It ensured that whenever AT&T needed a new integration or a tailored feature, the turnaround time was quick, bypassing typical vendor gatekeeping.
