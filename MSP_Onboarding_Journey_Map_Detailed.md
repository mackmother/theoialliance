---

## Strategic Foundation

Before diving into the map, here's the methodology being applied:

### Framework Sources

| Training Resource | What's Being Applied |
|-------------------|----------------------|
| Customer Onboarding (Irete Iseps) | "Onboarding is complete not when they're configured, but when they're seeing first value" |
| Nailing The Onramp (Dan Martell) | Three-circle model: Quick Win + Monitor + Invest = Activated Customer |
| Building Account Success Plans (Aaron Thompson) | Success = Desired Outcome + Desired Experience; milestone-based journey |
| Churn Buster Cheatsheet (Johnny Page) | "Increase commitment so clients take action" + handoff momentum principles |
| 3-Way Call Transcript | "Order today, trial next week, deploy this month, profit this quarter" |

---

## Design Principles

1. **First Value ≠ Setup Complete**
   - First value = MSP's first paying subscriber on wibipOS

2. **"Trial" Language**
   - Never "PoC" (validated in 3-way call)

3. **Multi-Stakeholder Reality**
   - MSP has technician, owner, and billing personas

4. **Hybrid Environment**
   - MSPs likely have legacy gear (Ruckus, Meraki) alongside trial kit

5. **Time-to-Revenue Focus**
   - MSPs care about profit, not features

---

## 🎯 The MSP Success Equation

### Desired Outcome
Deploy OpenWiFi profitably at a customer site within 30 days of kit arrival

### Desired Experience
- Feel supported, not abandoned
- Never feel "stuck" for more than 24 hours
- See clear path from trial → production → scale

### First Value Milestone
First end-customer invoice generated through wibipOS-managed network.

### Pre-Work Questionnaire (Critical Data Capture)

**"Help us help you deploy faster"**

1. **What's your first target deployment site?**
   - Type: MDU / SMB / Hospitality / Education

2. **How many APs do you anticipate for this site?**

3. **What legacy equipment exists today?**
   - Ruckus / Meraki / Aruba / Ubiquiti / None

4. **What's your current billing/PSA platform?**
   - ConnectWise / Autotask / HaloPSA / Other

5. **Who will be your technical lead for this trial?**
   - Name + Email + Phone

6. **What does success look like in 30 days?**

7. **Any hard deadlines we should know about?**

**Why This Matters:**
> "Most churn happens because there's a gap between the expectation the client had and what they actually got."

This questionnaire captures their definition of success before we impose ours.

### Kickoff Call Agenda (Day 5)

**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Attendees:** MSP Technical Lead + WiBUZ CSM + (optional) MSP Owner

**Agenda:**
1. (2 min) Introductions + confirm goals from questionnaire
2. (5 min) wibipOS dashboard tour—where to find what
3. (10 min) Live: Add first AP to wibipOS (screen share)
4. (5 min) Explain: NetExperience controller integration (if applicable)
5. (3 min) Discuss: Target deployment site—timeline and requirements
6. (5 min) Q&A + confirm next checkpoint

**Post-Call Deliverables:**
- Recording sent within 2 hours
- Action items documented in shared doc
- Next call scheduled (Day 10 Technical Validation)

**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Attendees:** MSP Technical Lead + MSP Owner (optional) + WiBUZ CSM

**Agenda:**
1. (5 min) Confirm target site details (from questionnaire)
2. (10 min) Network design review:
   - How many APs?
   - SSID strategy (guest vs. private vs. IoT?)

   - Captive portal needed?
3. (5 min) Integration planning:
   - Existing legacy gear on-site?
   - Billing/PSA integration timing?
4. (5 min) Timeline and go-live date
5. (5 min) Risk discussion: "What could go wrong? How do we prevent it?"

### NPS Survey (Day 35)

**Single Question + Follow-Up:**
> "On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend the Edgecore/wibipOS solution to another MSP?"

**Follow-Up Based on Score:**
- **Promoters (9-10):** "Amazing! Would you be open to sharing your experience as a case study or reference?"
- **Passives (7-8):** "Thanks! What would make this a 10 for you?"
- **Detractors (0-6):** "We're sorry to hear that. Can we schedule a call to understand what went wrong?"

**Detractor Response Protocol:**
- CSM reaches out within 24 hours
- Executive sponsor CC'd on escalations
- Root cause documented and fed back to product/operations

## Summary

The MSP Onboarding Journey Map provides a comprehensive, milestone-based framework for guiding managed service providers from initial trial commitment through long-term advocacy and expansion. Each of the five phases (Commit, Configure, Connect, Commercialize, Champion) is designed with specific emotional states, touchpoints, and success metrics to ensure MSPs achieve profitability within 30 days while building sustainable, mutually beneficial partnerships.

The framework emphasizes rapid value realization, continuous support, and psychological investment to transform trial customers into profitable advocates for the OpenWiFi solution.
