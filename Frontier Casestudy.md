Delivering Carrier-Grade Connectivity with Rapid Rollout
Frontier Communications needed to quickly extend high-performance Wi-Fi and networking services to commercial subscribers in 28+ states. From small local businesses and municipal buildings to large enterprise branches, Frontier aimed to offer flexible, branded services with quick turn-up and minimal complexity.

Working through Commscope’s managed services, Frontier utilized the wibipOS platform to unify the entire offering—from hardware provisioning and captive portals to advanced analytics—while offloading infrastructure hosting and day-to-day monitoring. This synergy allowed Frontier to focus on delivering sticky, recurring-revenue solutions without building everything from scratch.

Challenges
Diverse Footprint & Rapid Launch
With 430,000+ business customers, Frontier needed to roll out secure, high-speed Wi-Fi across numerous verticals (retail, small offices, hospitality, branch offices) efficiently.
White-Label, Carrier-Grade Services
Must brand the solution under Frontier’s name, offering enterprise-grade reliability and performance that smaller competitors lacked.
Minimize In-House Complexity
Frontier wanted to avoid assembling a patchwork of vendors and processes. They needed a singular, integrated approach for design, installation, NOC support, and future expansions.
The WiBUZ-Enabled Reseller/Frontier Solution
Ready-to-Deploy Technology + wibipOS Integration
The reseller supplied access points, switches, and the “Virtual Managed Edge” device. wibipOS served as the orchestration layer, seamlessly uniting provisioning, captive portal customization, and real-time analytics under a single pane of glass.
API-Driven, Modular Architecture
Through the API-first wibipOS core, the reseller's hosting environment and Frontier’s OSS/BSS systems communicated instantly. This lets Frontier create tailored packages—like cybersecurity add-ons or guest-employee SSID segmentation—without a massive rebuild.
Live SDK & Dynamic Roadmap
WiBUZ’s embedded “Live SDK” team worked with teh reseller to quickly configure new features or fix issues behind the scenes, accelerating time-to-market. Frontier could request a new SSID onboarding flow or brand-new features and see them deployed network-wide in record time.
Full-Service Implementation & NOC-to-NOC Support
While Frontier’s local technicians performed on-site installations, reseller’s NOC handled proactive monitoring. wibipOS provided advanced event visibility, enabling near-instant resolution. Frontier’s staff faced minimal overhead, focusing on sales and customer relationships.
Key Outcomes
Faster Rollouts Across 30+ States
Instead of building a managed Wi-Fi solution from scratch, Frontier leveraged the reseller + wibipOS to get a turnkey service ready for multiple verticals, reducing the overall deployment timeline.
Stronger, Stickier Services
Frontier quickly pivoted from one-time equipment sales to monthly recurring revenue on managed Wi-Fi, security, and networking offerings—backed by enterprise-grade reliability.
Holistic Management & Analytics
wibipOS gave Frontier robust analytics and straightforward management dashboards for network health, usage data, and real-time performance. This enhanced the user experience and deepened Frontier’s brand credibility.
Seamless Expansion & Differentiation
With everything integrated under one umbrella—hosted in reseller’s data center, orchestrated by wibipOS, and supported by WiBUZ’s Live SDK—Frontier could rapidly add new services like multi-site portal customizations or emerging IoT solutions.

By joining reseller’s fully managed approach with wibipOS at its core, Frontier swiftly introduced advanced, carrier-grade Wi-Fi and networking packages to SMB and enterprise markets. Moving beyond simple connectivity, Frontier can now offer robust solutions (like captive portals, enhanced security, and future IoT expansions) with minimal lead time, transforming one-off broadband customers into long-term managed services clients.
