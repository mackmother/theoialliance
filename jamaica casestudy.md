The Challenge
Jamaica’s Ministry of Education (MOE) needed to connect over 1,000 schools with reliable Wi-Fi—fast. But with multiple hardware vendors in play, the Ministry refused to be locked into a single ecosystem. They demanded:

Scalability – Thousands of APs nationwide, with future expansion on the horizon.
Multi-Vendor Flexibility – No forced forklift upgrades if a better vendor emerged.
Real-Time Oversight – Role-based dashboards for the Prime Minister’s Office, central IT, and local administrators.
Cost-Efficiency – Strict budget constraints, no endless R&D overhead.
Future-Ready – Ability to integrate emerging technologies (private 5G, IoT, advanced analytics) without ripping out the existing network.
